# Field Notes — Design direction

## Product surface

Course Notes is a read-first archive of local MDX study entries. Each note carries its title, source course, platform, topic, date, instructor, duration, level, and readable content. The collection has a full index and a detail route.

## Visual world

- **Seed:** \`b16140fa\`, assigned direction index \`5\` (\`operate\`)
- **World:** a field-note study desk: topics form the rail index, entries are numbered shelf records, and a detail route becomes a single paper note.
- **Palette:** warm paper \`#f3eee3\`, sheet white \`#fbf8ef\`, ink navy \`#222a35\`, archival red \`#a94739\`, and field blue \`#2e5871\`.
- **Typography:** Newsreader carries titles and long-form reading; DM Sans supports navigation; DM Mono handles labels, dates, and index facts.
- **Composition:** the home page starts with the archive thesis, then places a sticky topic rail beside a chronological list. The detail page removes collection noise and gives the note a broad, quiet reading sheet.
- **Interaction:** scan the topic rail, open a numbered note, and return to the index. The interface preserves source metadata rather than turning learning into generic category cards.
- **Responsive:** the topic rail becomes a preface above the list; note facts wrap into a two-column field grid; the reading measure remains constrained.
